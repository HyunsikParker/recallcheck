# Model weights

`onnx-community/dinov2-small/` holds the 8-bit ONNX export of DINOv2 ViT-S/14
(https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/dinov2-small), converted from
https://huggingface.co/facebook/dinov2-small. DINOv2 is released by Meta under the
Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The files are unmodified.

`tessdata/eng.traineddata.gz` is the Tesseract English LSTM model (`4.0.0_best_int`, from
https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best via the @tesseract.js-data/eng package), gzipped.
It is released under the Apache License 2.0.
