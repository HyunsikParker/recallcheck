The page reports how well this works. Tested on recall photos left out of the index, the right recall ranked first in fifty-four percent of searches, and made the top five in sixty-nine percent. On copies cropped, tilted and blurred to look like phone photos, those numbers fall to forty-seven and sixty-three percent, and the look-alike threshold is set on copies like these.
