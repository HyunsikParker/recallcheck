Most household product recalls list no barcode. For those, take a photo. An image model, DINO v two, runs inside the browser, so the photo is never uploaded. It compares the photo with nine thousand eight hundred and forty-five photos from C P S C recall notices since 2018. No recall photo looks much like this kettle, so the page says so and lists the five nearest recalls. Each one carries the notice's own description of which units are included. Not finding a match does not mean the item is safe, and the page says that too.
